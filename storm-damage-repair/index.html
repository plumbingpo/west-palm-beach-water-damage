How to Tell You Need Storm Damage Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Downspouts knocked off and gutter runs hanging after high wind
  • Water in the lowest level after storm drains back up or overflow
  • Standing water in attic spaces, especially by skylights and chimneys
  • New brown rings or damp marks on walls and ceilings once the storm passes
  • Damp, earthy odors that appear a few days after the storm
  • Bubbling paint and soft, drooping spots overhead once the rain stops
  • Flashing that is bent, loose or missing at roof edges, valleys and vents

Storm Damage Repair Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Trim trees near your home to lower the risk of wind-driven branch damage
  • Know how to shut off your main water supply in case a storm damages a pipe
  • Have your roof checked annually and fix vulnerable areas ahead of the stormy months
  • Extend downspouts and slope the soil so runoff moves away from the foundation
  • Take dated photos of the house every year, since insurers ask about prior condition
  • Make sure the attic is properly ventilated and insulated to prevent ice dams

Why Storm Damage Repair Is Not a DIY Job

The repairs a storm leaves behind are tangled together, and sorting them out correctly calls for an experienced restoration crew. Wet insulation, soaked roof decking and damp wall cavities often sit behind a leak that appears small, and without proper detection and drying they bring mold and a weakening structure. With moisture meters and thermal cameras, trained technicians follow the route water took and find all the areas it reached.

Emergency board-up and tarping also take proper technique to be effective. If a tarp is fastened poorly, it can direct water where you do not want it or give out when the wind picks up. Experienced in storm mitigation, our team picks appropriate methods and materials to keep the building protected until permanent repair work can be scheduled.
