Ceiling Water Damage Repair Help in West Palm Beach

A brown ring on the ceiling is the last thing to show up, not the first. Long before you see discoloration, the insulation is wet, moisture has followed the framing and a puddle sits on the hidden side of the panel. Common sources are the roof, an air conditioning drain, a pipe running overhead, or the bathroom one story up. Our team finds where it starts before any patching is done.

A coat of paint over the spot solves nothing. Moisture left in the cavity brings the discoloration back and lets mold get going on the paper side in 24 to 48 hours. Our crew removes no more ceiling than necessary, takes out soaked insulation, dries the framing and the drywall that stays, and finishes with a patch, texture and paint that match. Sagging changes the picture. Stay out from under it and call.

What It Looks Like

  • A smoke detector, register or fixture with water running from it
  • Ceiling texture crumbling off or changing color in one section
  • Discolored rings overhead that get bigger each time it rains or the bathroom is used
  • New fine cracks that radiate from a discolored patch
  • A musty smell in one room with no visible source
  • Seams, tape lines and fastener heads becoming visible across the ceiling
Our Process

What Happens After You Call

1

Find the Source

We track the water back to where it starts using moisture meters and thermal imaging, checking the room or attic above. The leak has to be stopped before repairs make sense.

2

Secure the Room

We cut power to any wet fixture and move or cover the furniture underneath. A ceiling with pooled water is relieved carefully, with the water collected as it comes out.

3

Open and Dry

Wet insulation is removed along with any drywall that cannot stay. The joists and nearby ceiling are dried with air movers plus a dehumidifier, and antimicrobial goes on wherever the water was dirty.

4

Rebuild and Paint

Once moisture readings are normal we install new insulation and drywall, tape and finish the seams, match the existing texture and prime with a stain blocker before painting.

If Nothing Is Done

How the Damage Progresses

First hours

Water Spreads Above

Water runs along joists and soaks into insulation. From inside the room everything can appear normal, since the drywall is taking on water on the hidden face.

Hours 24 to 48

Marks Start Showing

You see the first ring, along with blisters in the paint. The paper layer becomes a place mold is able to grow, and heavy wet insulation bears down from above.

Day 3 to 7

The Ceiling Bows

The panel turns soft and starts slipping off its fasteners. The ceiling bows between joists, seams open up and a musty odor reaches the room.

Past one week

Sections May Fall

Saturated pieces may drop and carry soaked insulation down too. By now mold has settled into the space above, and the area needing repair has grown considerably.

Hidden Risks

Risks That Are Easy to Miss

Damage on the Floor Above

The structure on the upper side, attic boards or the floor of the next story, tends to be wet too. Our inspection includes the space overhead, since moisture left there keeps feeding the problem.

A Ceiling That Lets Go

Wet drywall is far heavier than dry and loses its grip on the fasteners. Whatever sits under a swollen area is at risk if that section comes down.

Water Travels Sideways

Water follows joists, pipes and ducts, so it can drip several feet from where it entered. Cutting in just under the mark may leave damp areas elsewhere untouched.

Older Ceiling Materials

Some older popcorn textures and plasters contain asbestos. Testing comes first in such a home, ahead of any cutting, scraping or demolition of the damaged section.
