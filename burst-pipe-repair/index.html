A burst pipe is one of the most common water damage events a property can face, and potentially one of the most destructive. A single burst pipe can release 4 to 8 gallons of water per minute, which is 240 to 480 gallons per hour flooding through your walls, ceilings and floors. Pipes burst from freezing, old plumbing, too much water pressure, corrosion or a physical hit, and in every case a fast emergency response is what keeps water from ruining flooring, structural materials, building systems and your belongings.

Day or night, West Palm Beach Restoration responds to burst pipe emergencies across West Palm Beach and the nearby communities. We can help find the shutoff and stop the water, and our team works with licensed plumbers to see that the pipe itself is repaired correctly. Once the water source is stopped, we immediately begin extraction with truck-mounted and portable equipment, removing standing water from all affected areas as quickly as possible.

After the water is out, we dry every material that took on moisture: finished floors, subflooring, wood framing, insulation and drywall. Since water runs downhill through ceiling spaces, floor assemblies and wall cavities, a burst pipe frequently affects several rooms on more than one level. We trace where the water migrated with thermal cameras and moisture detection tools, find each affected area and dry it properly, heading off the mold and structural decay that follow hidden moisture.

Signs It Is Time for Burst Pipe Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Pipes coated in ice or frost when temperatures drop
  • Water charges that jump for no clear reason, a sign of a concealed leak
  • Faucets that give only a trickle, or nothing, during a freeze
  • Ceilings or walls that show damp patches, stains or a swollen bulge
  • Exposed pipes that drip, mist or shoot water
  • Water pressure that falls off abruptly at every fixture
  • Hearing water move through the house while nothing is turned on

Burst Pipe Repair Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Put leak sensors by the water heater and along the main supply line
  • Aging galvanized or polybutylene plumbing should be swapped out ahead of an unexpected failure
  • Every few years, ask a licensed plumber to look over the system for corrosion and worn parts
  • Everyone in the house should be able to locate and turn the main water valve in a hurry
  • Open sink cabinets and leave a slow drip running on cold nights, which helps keep pipes from freezing
  • Pressure higher than 80 PSI wears on pipes and their fittings, so test yours from time to time

The Benefits of Professional Burst Pipe Repair

What you can see after a pipe bursts is usually only part of the damage. Water travels through wall cavities, along electrical conduits, through floor assemblies and between ceiling joists, saturating materials in places you may not discover for days or weeks. By then, mold may already be established in hidden spaces and structural materials may be significantly weakened. With moisture meters and thermal cameras, trained technicians follow the water's path and pinpoint every affected area right away.

Professional-grade extraction and drying equipment also works on a completely different scale than consumer tools. Truck-mounted extractors remove water hundreds of times faster than a shop vacuum, and commercial dehumidifiers pull 10 to 20 times more moisture from the air than residential units. Mold can begin growing in 24 to 48 hours, so this kind of power and speed matters a great deal.
