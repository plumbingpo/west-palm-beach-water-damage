When to Call for Emergency Water Damage Restoration in West Palm Beach

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Sewage has backed up, or the water is dark with a foul odor.
  • Carpet, drywall or hardwood has been soaked for more than a few hours.
  • Pooled water is creeping over the floor into neighboring rooms or onto the staircase.
  • You see water escaping around a light fitting, from a wall outlet or through the ceiling.
  • A water heater tank has failed and is emptying across the floor.
  • Nobody was home when it started, so the length of the leak is unknown.
  • Trapped water overhead has made part of the ceiling bulge or sag.

Emergency Water Damage Restoration Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Insulate pipes in unheated spaces so they do not freeze and split in cold weather.
  • Sinks, dishwashers, washing machines and water heaters are all good spots for a leak sensor.
  • Before anything goes wrong, store the phone number for an emergency restoration crew.
  • Every member of the household should know where the main water valve is and how it closes.
  • Learn the location of your breaker panel, which lets you safely kill power to a wet room.
  • Test your sump pump before the rainy season and consider a battery backup.
