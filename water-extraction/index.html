Water extraction is the most critical first step in any water damage restoration project. The faster standing water is removed from your property, the less damage occurs to structural materials, flooring, personal belongings and building systems. Every hour that water sits on surfaces, it penetrates deeper into materials, soaking through carpet into padding, wicking up drywall, saturating wood framing and seeping into subflooring. For emergency water extraction in West Palm Beach, West Palm Beach Restoration brings truck-mounted units and portable extractors built to pull out hundreds of gallons every minute.

Truck-mounted units rank with the strongest water removal systems made, and their suction lifts water out of hard floors, carpet and structural materials much better than anything you can rent or buy. Where the truck hoses will not reach, we bring in submersible pumps, strong portable extractors and specialty tools made for water caught between floor layers, inside wall cavities or in tight spaces.

There is more to a professional extraction than getting rid of the water in plain sight. Using moisture detection equipment, our IICRC-certified team finds water that has moved out of sight, whether under cabinets, beneath flooring, behind baseboards or inside walls. Specialty extraction tools remove as much moisture as possible from these hidden areas before the drying phase begins, which significantly reduces the time and equipment needed for structural drying and lowers the risk of secondary mold growth.

Truck-mounted extractors move water at hundreds of times the rate of consumer tools, and a job that could cost you a full day with a shop vac is done by our machines in minutes. Our extraction also goes after moisture that home equipment can neither find nor get to. Through capillary action and wicking, water moves up drywall, into the pad under carpet, between layers of flooring and along plumbing lines and electrical conduit. Our specialty extraction tools and techniques target these hidden moisture deposits, removing significantly more water than surface extraction alone. This thorough approach reduces drying time, lowers equipment costs and, most importantly, prevents the hidden mold growth that results from trapped moisture.

Water Extraction Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Worn or aging hoses on washers, dishwashers and ice makers should be replaced before they fail
  • Keep a basic emergency water kit: old towels, a bucket and your restoration company's number
  • Leak-sensing shutoff valves can cut off the water on their own when something fails
  • Look beneath sinks, behind toilets and beside appliances every so often to catch leaks early
  • Keep valuables and stored boxes off the basement floor on shelves or pallets
  • Find the main shutoff valve for the house and put a clear label on it

Warning Signs for Water Extraction

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Ongoing leakage from the plumbing, the air conditioning or an appliance
  • Laminate or wood flooring that feels spongy and pushes up water at the seams
  • Any room where water has collected on the floor
  • A utility area, mechanical room or crawl space with water in it
  • Saturated carpet that squishes underfoot or shows water pooling
  • Stairwells or window wells that fill up and spill water inside
  • Rising water in the basement because the sump pump stopped working
