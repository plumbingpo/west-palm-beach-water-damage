Of all the problems an owner can have, sewage coming up through the drains ranks among the most dangerous. Raw sewage contains dangerous bacteria, viruses, parasites and other pathogens, including E. coli, hepatitis, salmonella and giardia, that pose serious health risks to anyone who touches contaminated materials. West Palm Beach Restoration provides emergency sewage cleanup throughout West Palm Beach, responding 24/7 with properly trained technicians, appropriate personal protective equipment and EPA-registered disinfectants to decontaminate your property safely.

Water damage comes in categories, and sewage sits in the worst one: Category 3, called black water, the highest level of contamination and danger. That rating calls for the strictest protocols: containment barriers go up, crews wear full protective equipment, every porous material the sewage touched is removed, and the surfaces that stay are disinfected thoroughly. For Category 3 water damage the reference is IICRC S500, and our crews follow it to bring the building back to safe occupancy.

Here is what the work covers: standing sewage is pumped out, contaminated water is extracted from the structure, porous materials the sewage touched (drywall, insulation, carpet and padding) are torn out and disposed of properly, hard surfaces are disinfected thoroughly, antimicrobials are applied, and the structure is dried so mold does not follow. We also deodorize the affected area with industrial-grade hydroxyl generators and thermal fogging equipment to eliminate lingering sewage odors.

How to Prevent Sewage Cleanup Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Keep cooking oil, grease and food scraps out of the drain, where they build into clogs
  • A drain that is slowing down should be dealt with early, while it is still partly open
  • Wipes clog sewer lines even when the label says flushable, so only toilet paper goes down the toilet
  • Protect against municipal sewer backups by having a backwater valve fitted where your line connects
  • Use drain strainers in showers and sinks to catch hair and debris
  • Clear tree roots near sewer lines, which can infiltrate and block the pipes

The Benefits of Professional Sewage Cleanup

Sewage cleanup is not something any homeowner should attempt. Handling raw sewage safely takes specialized training, EPA-registered disinfectants that consumers cannot buy, and personal protective equipment, meaning respirators, Tyvek suits and protective boots and gloves. People exposed to the pathogens in sewage can become seriously ill with skin and respiratory infections, hepatitis or gastrointestinal disease.

Disposal matters as well, since everything the sewage contaminated has to be handled as biohazard waste. Simply placing sewage-contaminated drywall, carpet and insulation in your regular trash is both illegal and hazardous to sanitation workers and the community. A certified restoration company follows proper disposal protocols and works with licensed facilities that accept biohazard waste.

Signs It Is Time for Sewage Cleanup

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • More than one drain in the house overflowing or backing up together
  • Gurgling sounds from toilets or drains when other fixtures are used
  • Sewage residue visible around drain openings or on basement floors
  • Unexplained illness or gastrointestinal symptoms among household members
  • Slow drains throughout the house that plunging does not fix
  • A rotten egg or sewer smell from the drains or the lowest floor of the home
  • Wastewater seeping from the sewer cleanout or pooling in the yard above the line
